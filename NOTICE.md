jsQR is the QR decoding engine used by the browser extension: it runs both in the page's
isolated world (`src/decode.js`) and inside the MV3 service worker.

## QR code test fixtures

`chrome-ext/test/fixtures.js` is generated by `chrome-ext/test/make_fixtures.py` using the
`qrcode` Python package (BSD-3-Clause). All links encoded in the fixtures are placeholders
(`https://pan.quark.cn/s/000000000000`, `aaa111`, `bbb222`) — no real share links.
